# Case Study 10: Karnataka Municipal Services Board

Industry: Public sector (state municipal services)
Headquarters: Bengaluru
Size: 8,600 employees
Service: Cloud Migration (phased) and Managed Cloud Support (Standard)
Cloud: Microsoft Azure (Central India and South India)
Engagement year: 2022 to 2023 (pre-dates the current rate card)

## Situation

The Board delivers property tax, trade licensing, and water billing
services through several older applications. A procurement rule required
that citizen data stay within India and that the engagement be delivered
through a formal tender process with fixed pricing. The Board wanted a
phased approach so that citizen-facing services stayed available.

## What we did

The estate was 72 servers migrated in two phases over a year, with
citizen-facing services (the tax and licensing portals) moved in the
second phase after the back-office systems had proven stable. We rehosted
58 servers and replatformed 14 workloads.

Delivery followed the Board's tender specification, including formal
acceptance milestones at the end of each phase and a documented rollback
plan for every cutover.

## Timeline

- Phase A (back office, 36 servers): 20 weeks including discovery and
  hypercare
- Phase B (citizen portals, 36 servers): 22 weeks including hypercare
- Total: 42 weeks across the two phases, with a 10-week pause between them
  for the Board's acceptance and budget cycle

## Commercials

Total fixed price approximately INR 31.5 lakh, priced under the rate card
in force at the time of the 2022 tender. Managed Cloud Support Standard
for 72 servers on a 24-month term.

## Outcomes

- Citizen-facing services recorded no unplanned downtime during the
  cutovers of the second phase.
- The Board's internal audit accepted both phases at the formal milestones
  without rework.
- Property tax portal response time under peak load improved from about
  6 seconds to about 1.8 seconds.
