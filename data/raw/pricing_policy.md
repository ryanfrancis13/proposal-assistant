# Meridian Cloudworks: Commercial and Pricing Policy

Document type: Pricing policy
Document version: 1.0
Status: Current
Effective from: 1 April 2026
Owner: Commercial Desk, Finance

## 1. Scope

This policy governs how quotes and proposals are priced. The standard rate
cards in the product documents are the only source of list prices. Prices
quoted in past case studies must never be presented as current prices.

## 2. Contractual discounts (fixed, not negotiable)

- A 24-month Managed Cloud Support term receives a 5 percent reduction in
  the monthly subscription.
- A Cloud Security Posture Service subscription bundled with Managed Cloud
  Support at the Standard or Premium tier receives a 10 percent reduction
  on the CSPS subscription.

Contractual discounts are applied first.

## 3. Negotiated discount

A customer may request a negotiated discount on the whole quote. It is
applied after contractual discounts. Approval depends on its size:

| Negotiated discount | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Up to 5 percent | None (sales manager discretion) |
| Above 5 and up to 10 percent | Regional Sales Head |
| Above 10 and up to 15 percent | Finance Director |
| Above 15 percent | Not permitted |

A request above 15 percent must be declined. The proposal may offer
alternatives such as a reduced scope, a phased engagement, or a longer
support term.

## 4. Price floor

After all discounts, the total must not fall below 80 percent of the
undiscounted list price. A negotiated discount that would breach the floor
must be reduced until it does not.

## 5. Price validity

A quote is valid for 30 days. After that it is re-issued at the rate card
then in force.

## 6. Payment terms

- Migration: 30 percent at kickoff, 50 percent on completion of the final
  migration wave, 20 percent at the end of Hypercare.
- Managed Cloud Support and CSPS: billed monthly in advance, payable
  within 30 days.
- All prices exclude GST. Cloud provider consumption charges are billed
  by the provider directly to the customer.

## 7. Commitments we do not make

- No guaranteed cloud cost reduction.
- No fixed price for refactoring work, which is a custom engagement.
- No price matching against other vendors.
