# Case Study 8: Prism Broadcast Network

Industry: Media and broadcasting
Headquarters: Mumbai
Size: 1,400 employees
Service: Cloud Migration (compact estate, fast timeline)
Cloud: Amazon Web Services (ap-south-1, Mumbai)
Engagement year: 2025

## Situation

Prism had to vacate its studio building's data room within 10 weeks
because of a landlord redevelopment notice. The estate covered a content
management system, an ad-scheduling platform, and archive storage for
broadcast metadata. There was no flexibility on the deadline.

## What we did

The estate was 24 servers. We compressed Discovery to 2 weeks by running
the automated scan and the application owner workshops concurrently, and
compressed the landing zone build to 2 weeks. All 24 servers were rehosted
because there was no time for replatforming, and the customer accepted that
some workloads would carry legacy configuration into the cloud.

Two parallel squads ran three waves. The customer received a list of
recommended post-migration modernisation items but chose not to buy
further services at that time.

## Timeline

- Week 1 to 2: Discovery and Assessment
- Week 3 to 4: Landing Zone Build
- Week 5 to 8: Three migration waves
- Week 9 to 12: Hypercare
- Total: 12 weeks from kickoff to end of hypercare. The data room was
  vacated in week 9, inside the landlord's deadline.

## Commercials

Total project fee approximately INR 13.4 lakh. This included a project
management uplift for the compressed timeline. No managed support
subscription was purchased.

## Outcomes

- The studio data room was vacated on schedule.
- All 24 servers migrated with no data loss.
- The customer's post-migration cloud cost was higher than its previous
  on-premises run cost for the first quarter because workloads were
  rehosted without optimisation. The modernisation list was intended to
  address this.

## Client quote

and it was met."
Chief Operating Officer, Prism Broadcast Network
