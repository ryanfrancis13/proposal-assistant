# Case Study 9: Fathom Analytics

Industry: Software as a service (B2B analytics)
Headquarters: Bengaluru
Size: 210 employees
Service: Managed Cloud Support (Standard) and Cloud Security Posture
Service
Cloud: Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure (multi-cloud)
Engagement year: 2024

## Situation

Fathom's enterprise customers, several of them global banks, required SOC 2
evidence and regular penetration test reports before signing or renewing.
The company's own engineering team of 45 was spending a fifth of its
capacity on operations and audit preparation rather than building product.
Fathom ran its production platform on AWS and an analytics workload on
Azure.

## What we did

We took over operations of 30 servers across the two clouds under Standard
support. The Cloud Security Posture Service covered 4 cloud accounts (3 AWS
accounts, 1 Azure subscription). The Compliance Reporting Pack was added
so that Fathom had quarterly control evidence ready for customer security
questionnaires. One external penetration test was purchased for the
customer-facing application.

## Timeline

- Week 1 to 2: Onboarding assessment
- Week 3 to 6: Remediation of findings and reporting set-up
- Steady state from week 7

## Commercials

Managed Cloud Support Standard for 30 servers on a 24-month term. CSPS for
4 accounts with the bundle discount. Compliance Reporting Pack
subscribed quarterly. One external penetration test.

## Outcomes

- Fathom's engineering team estimated it recovered about 15 percent of
  engineering capacity previously spent on operations.
- Customer security questionnaire turnaround fell from about 3 weeks to
  about 5 working days.
- Two enterprise renewals that had been contingent on updated pen test and
  control evidence were completed.

## Client quote

"Our customers ask hard security questions. Having evidence ready before
they ask turned a sales blocker into a routine."
VP Engineering, Fathom Analytics
