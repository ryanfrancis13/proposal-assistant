# Case Study 4: Tarang Logistics

Industry: Logistics and freight
Headquarters: Mumbai
Size: 5,200 employees, operations in 11 states
Service: Cloud Migration (large estate) with Managed Cloud Support
(Essential)
Cloud: Amazon Web Services (ap-south-1, Mumbai)
Engagement year: 2024

## Situation

Tarang operated its transport management system, warehouse management
system, and driver mobile back end from three data centres it had
accumulated through acquisitions. The estate was 155 servers with
inconsistent operating system versions, some past vendor end of support.
The company wanted to consolidate into one cloud environment and stop
paying for three data centre leases.

## What we did

Because of the size, Meridian ran two migration squads in parallel.
Discovery took three weeks and found 22 servers that were unused or
duplicated after the acquisitions, which were retired. Of the remaining 133
servers, 118 were rehosted and 15 workloads were replatformed.

Operating systems past end of support were upgraded during migration where
the application owner had signed off on the change, and otherwise were
migrated as-is and flagged in a risk register for the customer.

The migration ran in eight waves of about 16 servers each. Wave sequencing
followed the dependency map so that the transport management system and its
databases moved together.

## Timeline

- Week 1 to 3: Discovery and Assessment
- Week 4 to 5: Landing Zone Build
- Week 6 to 27: Eight migration waves, two squads in parallel
- Week 28 to 31: Hypercare
- Total: 31 weeks from kickoff to end of hypercare

## Commercials

Total project fee approximately INR 85.6 lakh, reflecting the larger estate
and the two-squad parallel delivery model. The customer took Managed Cloud
Support Essential for 133 servers on a 12-month term, approximately INR
38.3 lakh over the term. Combined programme value: approximately INR 123.9
lakh.

## Outcomes

- 133 servers were migrated and 22 retired. Two of the three data centres
  were closed within the engagement window; the third was closed four
  months later.
- Two waves needed a rehearsal re-run. Both were completed at no
  additional charge.
- Combined data centre lease and hardware maintenance costs fell by about
  INR 1.6 crore per year.
- The risk register carried 9 end-of-support items for the customer to
  address after migration.
