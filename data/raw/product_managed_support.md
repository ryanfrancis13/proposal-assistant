# Meridian Cloudworks: Managed Cloud Support

Document version: 3.7
Last reviewed: February 2026
Owner: Service Delivery

## 1. Overview

Managed Cloud Support is a monthly subscription in which Meridian monitors,
patches, secures, and supports a customer's cloud environment after
migration. It applies to AWS and Azure estates, including estates that
Meridian did not migrate, subject to a one-time onboarding assessment.

Support is delivered from our operations centre in Bengaluru with a
secondary centre in Pune.

## 2. Support tiers

Three tiers are offered. Prices are per server per month, excluding GST.

| | Essential | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per server per month (INR) | 2,400 | 3,900 | 6,200 |
| Coverage hours | 9x5, Mon to Fri | 16x6, Mon to Sat | 24x7x365 |
| Monitoring and alerting | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| OS patching | Quarterly | Monthly | Monthly, plus emergency out-of-cycle |
| Backup management | Verify only | Manage and test quarterly | Manage and test monthly |
| Security monitoring | Basic | Standard | Advanced with threat hunting |
| Named service manager | No | Shared | Dedicated |
| Monthly service review | No | Yes | Yes |
| Disaster recovery drills | No | Annual | Twice a year |

Minimum subscription: 20 servers. Minimum term: 12 months. A 24-month term
qualifies for a 5 percent reduction in the monthly subscription.

## 3. Response and resolution targets

Targets are measured from the time a ticket is logged.

| Severity | Definition | Essential | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Production down or data at risk | 2 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| Sev 2 | Major degradation | 8 hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| Sev 3 | Minor issue or request | 2 working days | 1 working day | 8 hours |

Resolution targets for Sev 1 are 8 hours for Essential, 6 hours for
Standard, and 4 hours for Premium.

## 4. Availability commitment

Meridian commits to a service availability target for the monitoring and
ticketing platform of 99.5 percent monthly (Essential), 99.7 percent
(Standard), and 99.9 percent (Premium). This is a commitment about
Meridian's operations platform. It is not a guarantee of uptime for the
customer's applications, which depends on the customer's architecture and
the cloud provider.

## 5. Service credits

If Meridian misses a Sev 1 response target, the customer receives a service
credit of 5 percent of that month's subscription for the affected servers,
up to a cap of 20 percent of the monthly subscription per month.

## 7. Certifications and audit

- ISO 27001:2022 certified.
- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA.
- PCI DSS: Meridian's operations centre is assessed as a service provider
  for customers that require PCI DSS aligned operations. Meridian does not
  hold a PCI DSS Level 1 attestation of its own.

## 8. Exclusions

Not covered: application-level support (custom code defects), end-user
device support, database performance tuning beyond configuration review,
and third-party software vendor support.
