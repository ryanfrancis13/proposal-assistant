# Case Study 7: Northgate Assurance

Industry: Insurance
Headquarters: Gurugram
Size: 2,900 employees
Service: Managed Cloud Support (Premium) and Cloud Security Posture
Service. No migration.
Cloud: Amazon Web Services (existing environment)
Engagement year: 2025

## Situation

Northgate had moved to AWS two years earlier using a different partner and
was unhappy with the support quality. Incidents were slow to resolve and
security findings were accumulating without owners. The company wanted a
new managed services provider without repeating the migration.

## What we did

Meridian ran an onboarding assessment of the existing 64-server estate
across 7 AWS accounts. It found configuration drift in the landing zone,
including several accounts without multi-factor authentication enforcement
and logging gaps in two accounts.

We onboarded the estate to Premium support and remediated the critical
findings during the first 60 days. Ongoing posture scanning covered all
7 accounts.

## Timeline

- Week 1 to 3: Onboarding assessment
- Week 4 to 9: Stabilisation and remediation of critical findings
- From week 10: Steady-state operation
- Total to steady state: 9 weeks

## Commercials

Onboarding assessment INR 2.1 lakh (one time). Managed Cloud Support
Premium for 64 servers on a 24-month term, approximately INR 90.5 lakh over
the term. CSPS for 7 accounts, with the bundle discount applied,
approximately INR 39.7 lakh over the term. Combined programme value,
excluding the onboarding assessment: approximately INR 130.2 lakh.

## Outcomes

- Mean time to resolve Sev 1 incidents fell from over 11 hours under the
  previous provider to under 3.5 hours over the first two quarters.
- The number of open high-risk security findings fell from 47 to 6 within
  90 days.
- Multi-factor authentication was enforced on all 7 accounts by day 30.
